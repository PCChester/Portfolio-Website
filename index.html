What I do — 3 phases

Built to work. Designed to stick.

Most AI projects fail not because the tools are wrong, but because nobody stayed to make sure the team understood them.

Phase 01
Audit & Strategy

Find where time is leaking

Map which 20% of tasks are worth automating first. After 30+ years of communication training, I speak the language of the C-suite and entry-level staff — and I deliver a clear picture, not a bloated proposal.

Phase 02
Build

Design and build the workflow or pipeline

You get a working tool, not a proposal. Python-backed, practical, built to last. Something tangible your team can point to and measure from day one.

Train & Enable

Stay until the team uses it confidently

That's when the ROI actually shows up. I don't hand over a tool and disappear — I stay until the team knows how to use it, adapt it, and trust it. Fear of new tools isn't a blocker; it's something I've been solving in classrooms and boardrooms for decades.

You walk away with:Custom prompt library · SOPs · Up to 3 training sessions
