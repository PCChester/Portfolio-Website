Train the Trainer, Dubai

A five-day programme for internal trainers, designed from scratch and delivered in July 2026.

What was built
  • A 102-page participant manual, with verified academic references throughout
  • A 199-slide deck covering all five days
  • Trainer notes for every session
  • Practical activities on learning design, delivery and managing a room
What made it different

Interactive, real-world practice from the first morning. Each participant worked on the doubts and weak spots in their own training, then delivered a session of their own and got individual feedback on it.
